Fusion versus IMU

Positive paired mean gain means EMG helped

Current conclusion

The continual-attention fusion model reached 122.2 px median at touch versus 126.4 px for IMU, but the paired mean gain was only +0.69 px. The temporal-touch five-fold study found a small, statistically positive +3.08 px mean gain for all causal EMG.
EMG-only remains much weaker. The evidence supports EMG as a small residual correction or gate on top of calibrated IMU—not as the primary endpoint sensor.
