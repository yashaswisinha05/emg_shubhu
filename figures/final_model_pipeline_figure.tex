    \caption{Overview of the proposed two-stage causal neuromuscular intent
    model. Raw four-channel sEMG and four six-axis IMU streams undergo causal
    filtering, validity-aware resampling, and training-only normalization. In
    Stage~I, a causal multiscale patch-transformer is trained for dense
    open/close recognition under auxiliary pose, screen-target, endpoint, and
    future-motion supervision. Its modality encoders and selected gripper
    classifier are then frozen. In Stage~II, two-layer causal GRU adapters reuse
    these shared features; detached state probabilities condition a bounded,
    zero-initialized sEMG correction to the adapted IMU mechanical
    representation. An EMG-only auxiliary decoder predicts future IMU interval
    summaries without a direct inertial shortcut. Separate heads estimate current Cartesian position,
    screen target, a dense 10--200~ms trajectory, and a low-rate 0.1--1.0~s
    intent trajectory. Dashed paths denote training-only objectives or frozen
    conditioning; no future measurement is used as an inference input.}
