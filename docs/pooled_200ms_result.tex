After pooling all valid frames from the 50 open-hand and 50 closed-hand trials,
the proposed model achieved the lowest 200-ms Cartesian prediction error of
8.04~cm, compared with 8.33~cm for IMU--GRU, 8.78~cm for IMU--LSTM,
11.73~cm for early-fusion sEMG+IMU--GRU, and 9.83~cm for early-fusion
sEMG+IMU--LSTM. This result shows that the proposed state-conditioned fusion
provides the strongest overall short-horizon generalization across both hand
states.
